# VISION X 2026 — From Curiosity to Impact

A React + Vite website for VISION X 2026, the Chitkara Research Club event scheduled for **27–28 November 2026** at **Chitkara University, Punjab**.

## Features

- Greek-inspired architectural identity blended with scientific and technology motifs.
- Responsive layouts and animated page transitions using Motion.
- Persistent Light / Dark theme switch.
- Live countdown to 27 November 2026 (India Standard Time).
- About VISION X: overview, draft vision and mission statements, and research context.
- Four competition pages: VOX SPHERE, INNOVISTA, BATTLE OF WITS and VISIONATHON.
- Interactive Day 01 / Day 02 schedule switcher.
- Prize pool overview showing 30K, with competition-wise details marked pending.
- Filterable gallery sections with clearly labelled media placeholders.
- Team, faculty coordination and volunteer information areas.
- Contact information and expandable FAQs.
- Existing Google registration form embed retained.

## Content notes

The source event brief does not provide confirmed schedule timings, venue rooms, speaker/judge profiles, competition-wise prize allocations, gallery assets, official email addresses or social handles. Those areas are marked as pending rather than populated with invented information. The vision and mission copy is labelled as draft editorial wording for review.
